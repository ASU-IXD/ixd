Designing Digital Worlds

Create immersive digital worlds using NVIDIA Omniverse, OpenUSD, AI‑assisted coding, simulation, and real‑time rendering — moving fluidly between speculative worldbuilding and precise digital twins.

Industry alignment

Where These Skills Are Used

OpenUSD and Omniverse workflows are already the connective tissue behind the studios, labs, and manufacturers building the world's next digital environments.

Course overview

Everything the pipeline runs on

Eight core capabilities, taught in sequence, that together let you author, script, connect, and present a complete digital world.

Course objectives

  • Use NVIDIA Omniverse for collaborative, high‑fidelity world creation across design disciplines.
  • Build foundational Python skills and AI‑assisted coding workflows for scripting and automation.
  • Learn Universal Scene Description as the shared language of 3D pipelines.
  • Integrate tools such as Revit, Blender, Unreal Engine, and Unity into one Omniverse pipeline.
  • Apply digital‑twinning principles to materials, lighting, physics, and real‑world conditions.

Student learning outcomes

  • Author and manipulate USD files to create, reference, and compose 3D scenes.
  • Write Python scripts and use AI‑assisted tools to automate Omniverse workflows.
  • Connect a primary design tool to an Omniverse pipeline via shared USD scenes.
  • Use real‑time rendering, physics simulation, and dynamic lighting in immersive environments.
  • Design and defend a world's position on the speculative‑to‑twin spectrum in critique.
Course framing

Speculative world ↔ Digital twin

Every project in this course lives somewhere on this spectrum. Drag the node to explore what changes at each position.

Speculative world Imaginative, narrative, experiential environments where story and atmosphere lead the process.
Digital twin Precise representations of real spaces, systems, and objects that simulate and inform decisions.
Hybrid

Students combine fiction and function, balancing conceptual worldbuilding with structured pipelines, collaborative review, and technical accuracy.
